### About

I'm a data engineer with ~5 years in the field, currently at **Scoro** working on the data
platform end to end — ingestion, warehousing, transformation with dbt, orchestration, and the
BI layer on top. I studied Computer Science at the University of Tartu, where my thesis was a
privacy-preserving image anonymisation tool.

Away from pipelines I run a homelab and self-host most of the things I use (this includes my
blog), follow Formula 1 a little too closely, and have a soft spot for drum & bass.
